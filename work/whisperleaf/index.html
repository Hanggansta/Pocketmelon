[[壹||01]][[命题||The brief]]

[[从教材到真正的原版书之间，隔着一堵墙。Whisperleaf 让读者留在原著里（盖茨比、奥斯汀、福尔摩斯），需要多少帮助就给多少，不多给一分。||English learners hit a wall between textbooks and real books. Whisperleaf lets them stay inside an original (Gatsby, Austen, Holmes) and get exactly as much help as they ask for, and nothing they didn’t.]]

[[难题||Challenge]]

[[查词工具把人拉出故事；背词软件又把单词从让人记住它的那个句子里剥离出来。||Look-up tools pull readers out of the story, and flashcard apps strip words of the sentence that made them memorable.]]

[[思路||Approach]]

[[一条不断的线：每一次查词、每一个收藏、每一张复习卡，都连着它出处的那段原文。||One continuous thread: every lookup, saved word and review card stays linked to the passage it came from.]]

[[原则||Principle]]

[[读者说了算。查一下不等于布置作业；只有你主动留下的，才会变成学习材料。||The reader is in charge. A lookup alone never becomes homework; only what you choose to keep becomes learning material.]]
