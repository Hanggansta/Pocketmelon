(01) The brief

English learners hit a wall between textbooks and real books. Whisperleaf lets them stay inside an original (Gatsby, Austen, Holmes) and get exactly as much help as they ask for, and nothing they didn’t.

Challenge

Look-up tools pull readers out of the story, and flashcard apps strip words of the sentence that made them memorable.

Approach

One continuous thread: every lookup, saved word and review card stays linked to the passage it came from.

Principle

The reader is in charge. A lookup alone never becomes homework; only what you choose to keep becomes learning material.

(02) Read · Keep · Return

01

Read

Bring an EPUB, a text-based PDF, a TXT file or a public article, or start from a curated shelf of classics. Choose your type, your reading mode and your pace.

  • EPUB
  • PDF · original & reading modes
  • TXT
  • Web articles

02

Understand

Look up a word, or select a sentence for XRay, an AI-assisted look at meaning and structure, with the surrounding passage still close.

  • Dictionary lookup
  • Sentence XRay
  • Context first

03

Keep

Save the words, passages and notes you want to learn from. Every one keeps its source, so practice can always lead back to the page.

  • Words
  • Passages
  • Notes & highlights

04

Return

Spaced review from your own material. Reveal the original context when you need it, then step straight back into the story.

  • Spaced review
  • Source-linked practice

(04) Craft notes

  • A literary identity

    A cloth-bound blue book with a blush spine, titled Between the lines, sets a calm tone that stays out of the reader’s way.

  • Private by default

    Original books, reading positions and learning plans stay on the device. Pro adds automatic private sync for what you save.

  • Honest pricing

    Free with a monthly AI allowance, Pro by the month or year, and no automatic charges, ever.
