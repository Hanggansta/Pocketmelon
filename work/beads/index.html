[[壹||01]][[命题||The brief]]

[[拼豆玩家习惯用方格纸、截图和猜测来做规划。口袋瓜拼豆在浏览器里给他们一间温暖可爱的工作室：收集灵感，转成拼豆图纸，创作像素风图案，然后带着图纸回到豆板前。||Bead makers plan with graph paper, screenshots and guesswork. 口袋瓜拼豆 gives them a warm, cute studio in the browser: gather inspiration, turn it into bead patterns and create pixel-style designs, then take the pattern to the pegboard.]]

[[难题||Challenge]]

[[图纸工具往往像电子表格。拼豆是一件温暖、动手的爱好，软件也该有同样的感觉。||Pattern tools tend to feel like spreadsheets. Bead making is a cosy, hands-on hobby, and the software should feel the same.]]

[[思路||Approach]]

[[奶油色的“手作桌”界面，一只有性格的吉祥物，和一张像真豆板一样的网格。||A soft cream “craft table” interface, a mascot with personality and a grid that behaves like a real pegboard.]]

[[原则||Principle]]

[[像素就是豆子。标志、图标、动效，全都画在玩家使用的同一张网格上。||The pixel is the bead. Everything (logo, icons, motion) is drawn on the same grid the maker works on.]]

[[肆||04]][[手记||Craft notes]]

  • [[是吉祥物，不只是标志||A mascot, not a logo]]

    [[口袋里的小猫撑起了整个品牌：它是应用图标，也是许多玩家拼的第一张图纸。||The cat in the pocket carries the brand: it appears in the app icon and it’s the first pattern many makers try.]]

  • [[处处像素精确||Pixel-true everywhere]]

    [[图标与插画都在拼豆网格上绘制，屏幕上看到的，就是你能拼出来的。||Icons and illustrations are authored on the bead grid, so what you see on screen is exactly what you can bead.]]

  • [[天生温暖||Warm by default]]

    [[奶油色（#fdf9ef）画布，柔和的珊瑚红与叶绿：是阳光下手作桌的配色，而不是设计软件。||A cream (#fdf9ef) canvas, soft coral and leaf green: the palette of a sunny craft table, not a design tool.]]
