[[壹||01]][[命题||The brief]]

[[拼豆玩家习惯用方格纸、截图和猜测来做规划。口袋瓜拼豆把这件事交给一台引擎：放进任何图像，它在毫秒内算出每一颗豆的位置和颜色，只用真实存在的豆色，然后让你带着图纸回到豆板前。||Bead makers plan with graph paper, screenshots and guesswork. 口袋瓜拼豆 hands that to an engine: drop in any image and it works out the position and colour of every bead in milliseconds, using only colours that really exist, then sends you back to the pegboard with the sheet.]]

[[难题||Challenge]]

[[RGB 距离挑出来的颜色常常“算对了却看错了”：肤色发灰，阴影变紫。量化必须按人眼的感知来做。||Matching colours by RGB distance is often right on paper and wrong to the eye: skin turns grey, shadows go purple. Quantisation has to follow perception.]]

[[思路||Approach]]

[[在 OKLab 感知色彩空间里，对 48 种真实豆色做最近邻匹配；整条管线跑在 GPU 上，拖动、缩放、换密度都是实时的。||Nearest-neighbour matching against 48 real bead colours in the OKLab perceptual space, with the whole pipeline on the GPU, so dragging, zooming and changing density all happen live.]]

[[原则||Principle]]

[[所见即所拼。屏幕上的每一格都对应豆板上的一颗豆，数量与色号可以直接拿去采购。||What you see is what you build. Every cell on screen is one bead on the board, and the counts and colour codes go straight onto a shopping list.]]

[[叁||03]][[工程手记||Engineering notes]]

  • [[按感知取色||Colour by perception]]

    [[颜色在 OKLab 中比较，明度权重 1.15。肤色保住温度，暗部不会被挑成紫色。||Colours are compared in OKLab with lightness weighted at 1.15, so skin keeps its warmth and shadows never get matched to purple.]]

  • [[一个着色器，三种状态||One shader, three states]]

    [[照片、平面图纸、熨烫后的豆子是同一个片元着色器的三个阶段，可以连续过渡，也可以用放大镜局部预览。||The photo, the flat pattern and the fused beads are three stages of one fragment shader. They blend continuously, and a lens previews the next stage locally.]]

  • [[只用真实存在的豆||Only beads that exist]]

    [[色板来自 48 种在售豆色，图纸里的每一种颜色都能买到、数得清。||The palette is 48 bead colours you can actually buy, so every colour in a pattern can be bought and counted.]]
