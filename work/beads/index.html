(01) The brief

Bead makers plan with graph paper, screenshots and guesswork. 口袋瓜拼豆 gives them a warm, cute studio in the browser: gather inspiration, turn it into bead patterns and create pixel-style designs, then take the pattern to the pegboard.

Challenge

Pattern tools tend to feel like spreadsheets. Bead making is a cosy, hands-on hobby, and the software should feel the same.

Approach

A soft cream “craft table” interface, a mascot with personality and a grid that behaves like a real pegboard.

Principle

The pixel is the bead. Everything (logo, icons, motion) is drawn on the same grid the maker works on.

(04) Craft notes

  • A mascot, not a logo

    The cat in the pocket carries the brand: it appears in the app icon and it’s the first pattern many makers try.

  • Pixel-true everywhere

    Icons and illustrations are authored on the bead grid, so what you see on screen is exactly what you can bead.

  • Warm by default

    A cream (#fdf9ef) canvas, soft coral and leaf green: the palette of a sunny craft table, not a design tool.
